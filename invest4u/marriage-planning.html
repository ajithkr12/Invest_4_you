What is it?

What is marriage planning?

Marriage planning means estimating what a wedding will cost when the time comes, and building a dedicated fund to pay for it, through regular saving and suitable investments.

It works like any other goal: a target amount, a date and a monthly plan to get there.

Why do I need it?

The risk marriage planning addresses

Weddings are among the largest expenses an Indian family faces, and costs rise every year. Without a plan, families often take loans or dip into retirement savings to pay for them.

Without it, you could face

  • High-interest loans for the wedding
  • Retirement savings used up early
  • Costs rising faster than expected
  • The plan stopping if a parent is no longer there

What marriage planning gives you

  • A realistic budget

    Today's costs projected forward, so the target is clear.

  • Steady saving

    Monthly SIPs sized to the years you have.

  • Goal protection

    Life cover so the plan continues even if a parent is not there.

  • Retirement kept safe

    A separate fund, so you do not have to break your retirement savings.

  • Safer as the date nears

    Money moved to lower-risk options in the final years.

  • Gold and jewellery

    A plan for jewellery purchases, staggered over time.
