What is it?

What is retirement planning?

Retirement planning means working out how much money you will need to live on after you stop working, and building a plan to get there: how much to save, where to invest it, and how to turn it into a regular income when you retire.

It also covers the protection you will need along the way, such as health cover and life insurance.

Why do I need it?

The risk retirement planning addresses

Most people in India do not have a pension. With longer lives and rising prices, your savings may need to last 25 years or more. Starting late, or without a plan, can mean running short just when you can no longer earn.

Without it, you could face

  • Savings running out in later years
  • Inflation steadily reducing what your money can buy
  • Medical costs rising as you get older
  • Depending on children for day-to-day expenses

What retirement planning gives you

  • Know your number

    An estimate of the corpus you need, allowing for inflation and how long you may live.

  • Save steadily

    SIPs and pension plans that grow your savings month by month.

  • Regular income

    Annuities and systematic withdrawal plans that pay you every month after retirement.

  • Health cover for later years

    A health policy that continues after your employer's cover ends.

  • Protect the plan

    Life cover so your spouse is looked after if something happens to you.

  • Nominations in order

    Up-to-date nominations on every policy and investment.
