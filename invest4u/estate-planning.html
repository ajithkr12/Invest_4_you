What is it?

What is estate planning?

Estate planning means deciding who should receive your assets after you, and making sure the paperwork, nominations, will and records, makes that happen smoothly.

It also covers providing your family with ready money and clear information at a difficult time.

This page is general information, not legal or tax advice. Wills, trusts and succession arrangements should be prepared with a qualified lawyer; we work alongside your lawyer and chartered accountant.

Why do I need it?

The risk estate planning addresses

Without a plan, families can face months of paperwork, frozen accounts and sometimes disputes, just when they need support. Simple steps taken today can save your family a great deal of stress.

Without it, you could face

  • Assets stuck without a nominee or will
  • Disputes between family members
  • Your family unaware of what you own
  • No ready money while assets are transferred

What estate planning gives you

  • Correct nominations

    Every policy and investment with an up-to-date nominee.

  • A clear will

    Pointers on making a will with a lawyer, so your wishes are followed.

  • Organised records

    One place where your family can find every asset and contact.

  • Ready money

    Life insurance that gives your family cash while other assets are transferred.

  • Fewer disputes

    Clear instructions reduce the chance of family disagreements.

  • Regular updates

    Reviews after marriage, a birth, a death or a major purchase.
