What is it?

What is goal-based planning?

Goal-based planning starts with what you want to achieve, not with products. Each goal gets a target amount, a date and its own savings and investment plan.

It brings your insurance, savings and investments together into one plan that you can track.

Why do I need it?

The risk goal-based planning addresses

Without clear goals, money tends to be saved randomly and spent on whatever comes up. Linking every rupee to a purpose makes it far more likely that you reach the goals that matter most.

Without it, you could face

  • Saving without knowing if it is enough
  • One goal crowding out another
  • Investments that do not match the time frame
  • Important goals left unfunded

What goal-based planning gives you

  • Clear priorities

    Every goal listed, costed and ranked.

  • Separate buckets

    Money for each goal kept and invested separately.

  • Real numbers

    Future costs that allow for inflation.

  • The right risk

    Short-term goals in safer options, long-term goals in growth assets.

  • Protected plan

    Life and health cover so one event does not undo the plan.

  • Annual review

    Progress tracked and the plan updated each year.

How can Invest 4U help?

Consultation, comparison, planning and ongoing support

In a free consultation we list your goals together: a home, children's education and weddings, retirement, travel or a business. We put a cost and a date on each, and check what you already have.

Then we build one joined-up plan: how much to save for each goal, where to invest it, and the protection needed so a setback does not derail it. We review it with you every year.
