About Us

Guiding Investors for Over Three Decades

Invest 4U began in 1992 in Tripunithura with one goal: to help local families protect what they have and plan for what they want. For more than three decades we have been a trusted partner of LIC of India, and today we also advise on health and corporate insurance, mutual funds and long-term financial planning.

In 2025 we formally registered as K & VK Invest 4U Advisory Services LLP. Our approach hasn't changed: service first, with time-tested solutions for short and long-term goals, and support that continues long after the policy or investment is in place.

  • Lifelong service
  • Claim support
