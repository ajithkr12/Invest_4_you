About the FD calculator

A fixed deposit (FD) earns a fixed rate of interest for a set period. Most Indian banks compound FD interest quarterly, which this calculator assumes.

Interest on FDs is taxable at your income-tax slab rate, and banks may deduct TDS.

How to use it

  1. Move the sliders, or type a value into each box.
  2. Watch the results and chart update as you go.
  3. Try different assumptions to see what changes the outcome most, then talk to us about a plan.

The formula

A = P × (1 + r ÷ 4)4t

  • P: deposit amount
  • r: annual interest rate
  • t: time in years (compounded quarterly)

Example

A deposit of ₹1,00,000 for 5 years at 7% would mature at about ₹1,41,478.
