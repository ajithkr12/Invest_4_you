About the Retirement calculator

Your retirement savings may need to last 25 years or more, while prices keep rising. This calculator estimates the corpus you need on the day you retire to cover your expenses, rising with inflation, until the age you plan for.

It then shows how much your current savings could grow to, and the monthly SIP needed to close any gap.

How to use it

  1. Move the sliders, or type a value into each box.
  2. Watch the results and chart update as you go.
  3. Try different assumptions to see what changes the outcome most, then talk to us about a plan.

The formula

Corpus = yearly expenses at retirement × [ 1 − (1 + g)−N ] ÷ g × (1 + g)

  • g: real return after retirement, (1 + return) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1
  • N: years in retirement
  • Expenses at retirement = today's expenses × (1 + inflation)years to retirement

Example

Aged 35 with monthly expenses of ₹40,000, retiring at 60 and planning until 85 (6% inflation, 11% return before and 7% after retirement): you may need a corpus of about ₹4,61,19,865. With ₹5,00,000 saved already, a SIP of about ₹24,725 a month could close the gap.
