What is it?

What is wealth transfer?

Wealth transfer is how your money and assets pass to the next generation, through gifts during your lifetime, investments in their names, life insurance payouts, nominations and your will.

Planning it means your family receives what you intend, at the right time, with as little cost and delay as possible.

This page is general information, not legal or tax advice. Wills, trusts and succession arrangements should be prepared with a qualified lawyer; we work alongside your lawyer and chartered accountant.

Why do I need it?

The risk wealth transfer addresses

Without a plan, wealth can be delayed, reduced by avoidable costs, or divided in ways you did not intend. A little planning makes sure your hard work benefits the people you care about.

Without it, you could face

  • Wealth reaching heirs slowly or not as intended
  • Avoidable costs and paperwork
  • Unequal treatment of children by accident
  • Your own retirement left short by giving too early

What wealth transfer gives you

  • Insurance as a transfer tool

    A life policy creates a defined sum for your heirs.

  • Lifetime gifts

    Helping children and grandchildren while you can see the benefit.

  • Smooth handover

    Nominations and ownership arranged so money reaches heirs quickly.

  • Investments for the next generation

    SIPs and plans for children's and grandchildren's goals.

  • Fair between heirs

    A plan that treats everyone as you intend.

  • Paperwork in order

    Records and nominations that match your wishes.
